## Projekt 5 – Automatisierung

**Project name** (max. 50)
```
Concept: Lead-to-CRM Automation (n8n)
```
**Industry:** Business Services / Marketing / Technology
**Project cost (falls Pflicht):** 59
**Bilder:** `5-automation-1.png`, `5-automation-2.png`, `5-automation-3.png`

**Project description** (max. 1.400)
```
Example workflow for a fictional interior design studio, built to show how I automate repetitive business tasks. The full workflow is built in n8n and can be rebuilt in Make or Zapier.

The brief: every website enquiry was copied into a spreadsheet by hand, replies were slow, and follow-ups were often forgotten.

What the automation does:
• Receives every new website form submission instantly (webhook)
• Cleans up the data: fixes name capitalization and email formatting, and stops invalid entries
• Adds the lead as a new row in Google Sheets (works the same with HubSpot, Airtable or Notion)
• Sends a personal welcome email to the lead
• Notifies the team in Slack
• Waits 2 days, checks for a reply and sends a friendly follow-up only if the lead has not answered

I also built an interactive demo page that simulates the workflow step by step, plus a ready-to-import n8n file.

Challenge: follow-ups must never go to people who already replied. The workflow checks the inbox before sending, so every lead gets exactly the right message.

Want to stop copy-pasting? Send me a message describing your process.
```
