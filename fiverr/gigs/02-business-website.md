### FAQ
```
Q: Can you redesign my existing website?
A: Yes. Send me the link and I will use your current content as a starting point.

Q: Can I update the website myself?
A: Text and image changes are simple, and I include a short guide. Or add the monthly maintenance extra and I make updates for you.

Q: Do I need hosting?
A: You can use your existing hosting, or I set it up on a fast, low-cost host like Netlify or Vercel and connect your domain.

Q: Do you build on WordPress or Wix?
A: I build custom-coded websites for best speed and security. If you need a specific platform, please message me first.

Q: Will my website show up on Google?
A: Standard and Premium include SEO basics (titles, descriptions, sitemap, speed). Ranking also depends on your Google Business Profile and reviews, and I can give you tips for both.
```

### Anforderungen an den Käufer (Requirements)
```
1. Business name, what you offer and your location.
2. Which pages do you need? (e.g. Home, Services, About, Prices, Contact)
3. Please upload your logo, brand colors and photos (if you have them).
4. Please paste or upload your text (Complete package: bullet points are enough).
5. Contact details to show: phone, email, address, opening hours.
6. Where should form submissions go? (email address)
7. Links to 1–3 websites you like (optional).
8. Do you already have a domain and hosting? If yes, which provider?
```
