### FAQ
```
Q: Do I need a Shopify account first?
A: Yes, please create your store (Shopify offers a free trial). I send you a collaborator request, so you never need to share your password.

Q: Do you use a free or paid theme?
A: Either. Free themes work great for most stores. If you buy a premium theme, I set it up for you.

Q: Can you add my products from a spreadsheet?
A: Yes. Send me a CSV or Excel file with your products and I import and tidy them up.

Q: Do you help with apps (reviews, email, upsells)?
A: Yes, I can recommend and install apps. App subscription costs are paid by you.

Q: Will my store be ready to sell?
A: Launch and Pro include payment and shipping setup. Things only you can do, like verifying your identity or bank account with Shopify, I explain step by step.
```

### Anforderungen an den Käufer (Requirements)

1. Your store URL (yourstore.myshopify.com).

5. Which countries do you ship to, and what are your shipping prices?
6. Links to 1–3 stores you like (optional).
7. Do you already have a theme in mind?
