# Nachrichten-Vorlagen für Kunden

> **Für dich (Deutsch):** Für die häufigsten Situationen. Kopieren, `[Klammern]` ausfüllen, abschicken.
> **Noch einfacher:** Kopier mir die Nachricht des Kunden in den Chat
> („Kunde schreibt: …“). Ich schreibe dir dann die passende Antwort.
>
> **Gut zu wissen:** Wie schnell du antwortest, beeinflusst dein Ranking auf Fiverr.
> Installier die Fiverr-App, damit du Benachrichtigungen bekommst, und antworte möglichst innerhalb von 1–2 Stunden.

---

## 1. Erste Anfrage: Rückfragen stellen
*Wenn jemand fragt „Can you do X?“ und noch unklar ist, was genau gebraucht wird.*
```
Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out!

Yes, I can help with that. To give you an exact offer, could you tell me:
1. What is the main goal? (e.g. more bookings, selling a product, collecting emails)
2. Do you already have text, logo and images, or do you need help with those?
3. Is there a website you like the style of?
4. When do you need it finished?

As soon as I have this, I will send you a clear offer with price and delivery date.
```

## 2. Individuelles Angebot (Custom Offer)
*Zusammen mit dem Custom Offer in Fiverr verschicken.*
```
Thanks for the details, [Name]! Here is what I suggest:

• [Leistung 1, z. B. one-page landing page with 6 sections]
• [Leistung 2, z. B. contact form sending to your email]
• [Leistung 3, z. B. mobile and speed optimization]
• [X] revisions included

Delivery: [X] days. Price: $[X].

I have sent you the offer. Once you accept, I will start right away and send you a first preview.
```

## 3. Kunde will telefonieren oder einen Video-Call
*Freundlich ablehnen und schriftlich weitermachen.*
```
Thanks for offering a call! I work best in writing. It keeps every detail documented so nothing gets lost, and you can reply whenever it suits you.

Feel free to send me everything here, even voice notes or screenshots are fine. I will reply with a clear written plan, and you will get a preview link early so you can see the progress.
```
> Sprachnachrichten des Kunden kannst du dir anhören, ohne selbst zu reden. Den Inhalt kopierst du mir in den Chat.

## 4. Auftrag gestartet
```
Hi [Name], thanks for your order! I have everything I need and I have started working on it.

You will get a first preview by [Datum/Tag]. If anything comes to mind in the meantime, just send it here.
```

## 5. Infos fehlen noch
```
Hi [Name], I am ready to start! I am only missing:
• [fehlende Info 1]
• [fehlende Info 2]

As soon as I have these, I will get going. If you are not sure about any of them, just tell me and I will suggest something.
```

## 6. Zwischenstand / Vorschau
```
Hi [Name], here is the first version for you to review: [Vorschau-Link oder Screenshot]

Please have a look and tell me what you like and what you want changed. A simple list is perfect. You have [X] revisions included.
```

## 7. Lieferung
*Bei „Deliver Now“ in Fiverr einfügen.*
```
Hi [Name], your [project] is ready! 🎉

Included in this delivery:
• [Dateien / Link]
• [Kurze Anleitung zum Online-Stellen / Bearbeiten]

Please check everything. If anything needs adjusting, use the revision request and I will take care of it.

It was great working with you. If you are happy with the result, I would really appreciate your honest feedback on Fiverr.
```
> ⚠️ Fiverr-Regel: Du darfst **nicht** um 5 Sterne bitten und keine Belohnung für Bewertungen anbieten.
> „Honest feedback“ ist erlaubt.

## 8. Änderungswunsch (Revision)
```
Thanks for the feedback, [Name]! I will make these changes:
• [Änderung 1]
• [Änderung 2]

You will get the updated version by [Tag].
```

## 9. Wunsch außerhalb des Auftrags (Zusatzverkauf)
```
Good idea! This goes beyond the original order ([was vereinbart war]), so I have sent you a small extra offer for it: $[X], [X] extra days.

If you prefer to keep the original scope, no problem. Just let me know.
```

## 10. Kunde will außerhalb von Fiverr bezahlen oder schreiben
*Immer ablehnen, sonst riskierst du die Sperre deines Kontos.*
```
Thanks for the trust! To keep things safe and simple for both of us, I keep all communication and payments here on Fiverr. I will send you a custom offer here.
```

## 11. Verspätung
*Lieber früh Bescheid geben als zu spät.*
```
Hi [Name], a quick update: I need [1 more day] to finish [project] properly, because [kurzer ehrlicher Grund]. You will have it by [Datum]. Sorry for the delay, and thanks for your patience!
```

## 12. Auftrag ablehnen (passt nicht)
```
Hi [Name], thanks for thinking of me! Unfortunately this is not something I can deliver at the quality you deserve, so I would rather not take it on. I wish you the best of luck with the project!
```

## 13. Nach ein paar Wochen: Pflege anbieten
```
Hi [Name], I hope your [website/shop] is running well! If you ever need updates (new text, prices, photos, sections), I offer monthly maintenance for $[X]/month, so you never have to worry about it. Just let me know if that is interesting for you.
```
