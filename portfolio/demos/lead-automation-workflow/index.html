EXAMPLE Lead → CRM → Follow-up automation

Every new lead handled in seconds — automatically.

A website form fills an inbox. Someone copies each lead into a spreadsheet, writes a reply when they get a moment, tells the team, and tries to remember to follow up. Replies get slow, details get lost, and good leads go cold.

This workflow does all of it the moment the form is submitted — built in n8n, and just as buildable in Make or Zapier.
