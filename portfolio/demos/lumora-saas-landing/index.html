How does Lumora join my meetings?

Connect your calendar and choose which meetings Lumora should attend. It joins as a clearly named participant, announces that it’s taking notes, and leaves when the meeting ends. For in-person meetings, just tap record in the mobile or desktop app.

Is my meeting data used to train AI models?

No. Your recordings, transcripts and summaries are never used to train models — ours or anyone else’s. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can delete any meeting permanently at any time.

Which languages does Lumora support?

Lumora transcribes more than 40 languages and can summarize in a different language than the one spoken — handy for distributed teams. It also handles mixed-language meetings.

Do participants know they’re being recorded?

Always. Lumora introduces itself when it joins, shows a visible recording indicator, and lets any participant pause or remove it. Admins can also require consent prompts for external guests.

How does the free trial work?

You get every Team feature for 14 days, no credit card required. At the end of the trial you can pick a paid plan or drop to Starter — your notes stay either way.

Can I change plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your workspace settings in two clicks. Yearly plans are prorated, and you can export all of your notes whenever you like.
