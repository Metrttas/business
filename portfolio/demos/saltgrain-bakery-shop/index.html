    Our story

    A tiny bakery with a very old starter.

    Saltgrain began in 2014 with a borrowed deck oven, a sack of stone-milled flour and a jar of sourdough starter we named Old Gus. We still feed him twice a day.

    Every loaf is mixed by hand, rested for two nights and baked hot on stone before the street wakes up. No shortcuts, no preservatives — just flour, water, salt and time.

    “Good bread is mostly patience. The rest is butter.”
    — Mara & Theo, founders
    • 11 yrsof feeding Old Gus, our starter
    • 2,400loaves shaped by hand each week
    • 0preservatives or improvers. Ever.
    1. 01 Day one · 2pm

      Mix & rest

      Stone-milled flour, water, sea salt and a scoop of Old Gus. Then a long, cold, 48-hour nap.

    2. 02 Day three · 4am

      Shape & bake

      Hand-shaped, scored with a razor and baked hot on stone until the crust sings as it cools.

    3. 03 By 9am

      To your door

      Wrapped in compostable paper and pedalled over by cargo bike, often still a little warm.
