Why patients choose us

A dental studio built around how you feel, not how fast we can go.

Dr. Elena Marsh opened Harborview in 2008 with a simple idea: if people dread the dentist, the dentist should change. Today our small team still books fewer patients per day than most practices, so there is always time to listen.
